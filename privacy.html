1. About this policy

Peermigo is a student networking and study collaboration platform. This policy explains how personal information may be processed when you visit or use Peermigo, including when you create a profile, message another student, share notes, or join a group.

For questions or privacy requests, email peermatchstudents@gmail.com. We aim to handle personal information responsibly in line with applicable South African privacy requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use Peermigo, information may include:

  • Account data: email address, authentication details and account identifiers. Passwords are processed through the authentication provider, not stored in plain text by the website.
  • Profile data: name, institution, campus, course, modules, study preferences, about text, profile picture, verification status and discovery settings.
  • Content and activity: messages, group membership, discussions, scheduled sessions, RSVPs, notes, saved-item bookmarks, uploaded files, reports and blocks.
  • Service information: notification choices, optional last-seen information, session and security logs, and technical data needed to maintain the service.

Do not upload sensitive personal information, identity documents or private contact details into public posts or group spaces. If proof of study is requested for manual verification, contact us first about a suitable private submission method.

3. How we use your information

Information is used to operate accounts and sign-in, help students find relevant peers, provide messaging and study-group features, deliver requested notifications, support manual student verification, maintain safety controls, investigate reports, and protect the service from abuse.

Optional activity emails are sent according to each student's notification preferences. Account security and password-recovery messages are handled separately.

4. Who can see your information

When you create an account through the registration form, you provide your name, institution and either a course/programme or school grade. Campus is optional; subjects and modules can be added later. Peermigo creates a student profile from those details. By default, other signed-in students can discover your profile, but not your sign-in email address. You can opt out of discovery during registration. You can turn off profile discovery in your profile privacy settings. When enabled, other signed-in students may see the profile details you add, your profile picture, and public study notes. Your institution, course or grade, and any study interests you later add may be visible as part of matching and profile viewing.

Your saved-item list is only visible to your signed-in account. Private notes and their attachments are intended for the author; direct-message attachments are intended for conversation participants; study-group material is intended for authorized group participants. Group and session discovery may expose limited information to other signed-in students.

Profile discovery and last-seen display can be controlled through the available profile settings. Changing visibility may not immediately invalidate an already issued, short-lived download link.

5. Service providers and international processing

Peermigo currently uses third-party services to operate. These include Supabase for authentication, database, file storage and backend functions; Netlify for website hosting; and Brevo for optional activity emails. These providers may process data on servers outside South Africa.

We do not offer students' personal information for sale. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to address abuse, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Storage, security and retention

Peermigo uses access controls, including Supabase Row Level Security and private file storage rules, to restrict access to content. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Information is kept while needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, respond to safety issues, or comply with applicable obligations. Deleting content from the interface may not immediately remove it from backups, security logs, or copies saved by other users. Retention periods and backup arrangements may change as the service develops.

7. Your choices and rights

Where available in Peermigo, you can edit your profile, change profile discovery and activity status, edit or delete your notes, leave groups, block users and update optional email notification preferences.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to ask about closing an account, contact peermatchstudents@gmail.com. We may need to confirm that the request relates to your account. Applicable legal and safety requirements may affect what we can remove.

8. Browser storage and external sites

Peermigo uses browser storage for preferences such as light/dark theme and may rely on authentication session storage to keep you signed in. Hosting and service providers may also use technical logs and security mechanisms.

The Resources page links to independently operated websites. Their content and privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated date above shows when this text was last revised. Important changes may be communicated through the website.

Email peermatchstudents@gmail.com for any questions about this policy.
