Community Guidelines.

Simple expectations that help every student feel respected, safe and welcome in Peermigo.

1. Respect other students

Speak to people with respect, even when you disagree. No harassment, bullying, threats, hateful comments, sexual pressure or repeated unwanted contact. Respect boundaries and stop messaging when someone asks you to stop.

2. Keep content useful and genuine

Share thoughtful notes, ask clear questions and credit your sources. Do not post misleading claims about qualifications or institutions, spam, scams, cheating-for-hire offers, or unrelated promotions.

3. Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's email address, phone number, location, photographs, ID documents or private chats without consent. Never ask for passwords or authentication codes. Use private messages responsibly and remember recipients can save what you share.

4. Upload responsibly

Only share files and images you are allowed to distribute. No malware, pirated course content, non-consensual intimate images, or illegal materials. Clearly title notes and group attachments so others know what they're opening.

5. Make in-person sessions safer

Prefer public, familiar locations, tell someone where you're going, and avoid sharing more personal information than necessary. Trust your judgment about meeting someone offline. Peermigo does not background-check or supervise participants.

6. For group administrators

Set clear group purposes, remove abusive posts when necessary, treat members fairly, and avoid using member-removal powers to harass or retaliate. Do not misrepresent admin permissions as official endorsement.

7. Report concerns

Use in-app block or report features when available, or email peermatchstudents@gmail.com with the relevant details. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on a platform report.

8. What happens after a report

We may review reports, request more information, remove content or limit accounts depending on the seriousness of the issue. Reporting does not guarantee immediate action. These guidelines work alongside our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
