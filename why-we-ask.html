Why do we ask for your study details?

Peermigo connects students for meaningful study partnerships. A few essentials help people find relevant peers before anyone sends a message.

THE ESSENTIALS

How each detail helps

These details help your profile be useful from day one, without sharing your email publicly.

01

First name and surname

Helps study partners recognise who they're talking to. You can include additional given names or surnames; we display them together as your profile name.

02

Institution and type

Helps students find others at their university, college or school. Campus is optional for university and college students and isn't required for school learners.

03

Course or school grade

Helps Peermigo suggest students at a similar stage of study. School learners choose a grade rather than having to enter a university programme.

04

Subjects or modules (optional)

Sharing your subjects or modules can help improve study matches. This field is optional at registration, and you can add or change it later in Edit Profile.

05

Email and password

Used to create and secure your account, confirm your email, and support account recovery. Your password is handled through Supabase Authentication.

YOUR PROFILE, YOUR CONTROL

You can update your study details.

Subjects and modules can be added later. You can also update your information, upload a profile photo and set study preferences through Edit Profile.

Who can see these details?

Student profiles are available to signed-in Peermigo members according to your account's access and discovery settings. If you choose to appear in Find Peers, other signed-in students may see your name, institution, course or grade, and subjects or modules. You can switch off profile discovery during registration or later in your profile settings.

Important: Entering a school or university name does not verify enrolment. Peermigo's verified badge is separate. Peermigo currently accepts students aged 18 and older only, including those who select School.
