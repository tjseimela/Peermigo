LEARN SMARTER

Study methods that actually help

Small changes to your study routine can make a big difference.

FOCUS

The Pomodoro technique

Work in focused 25-minute blocks, then take a five-minute break. A simple way to avoid burnout and stay on track.

25 min focus · 5 min rest
MEMORY

Active recall

Put your notes away and test what you remember. Retrieving information helps you learn more effectively.

Learn it · Recall it · Repeat
RETENTION

Spaced repetition

Revisit important topics at increasing intervals to keep them fresh in your memory for longer.

Review · Reinforce · Remember
