1. Using Peermigo

By creating an account or using Peermigo, you agree to these Terms of Use and our Community Guidelines. Please also read our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, do not use the service.

Peermigo is a student community platform for discovery, study collaboration, notes, messaging and group sessions. Features may change while the platform develops.

2. Eligibility and account security

Peermigo is intended only for users aged 18 or older. Provide accurate account and profile information, keep your sign-in details confidential, and do not use another person's account without permission.

Do not impersonate another student, falsely claim attendance at an institution, or attempt to grant yourself a verified status. A Verified Student badge represents a manual review at a point in time; it is not a guarantee of identity, safety or academic status.

3. Respect and safety

Treat people respectfully. Harassment, threats, unwanted sexual content, hate speech, bullying, stalking, scams, spam, unauthorized advertising, and attempts to obtain another person's private information are not permitted.

Use the platform's blocking and reporting tools where available. For in-person study sessions, choose safe public locations and use your own judgment. Peermigo does not supervise face-to-face meetings.

4. Notes, messages and uploads

You remain responsible for content you post or send, including notes, messages, images, documents and study resources. Only upload material you have the right to share. Do not distribute copyrighted textbooks or paid course materials without authorization, malicious files, unlawful content or private information about others.

You retain ownership of your original content. You give Peermigo the limited permission needed to store, process and display it to the audience you select so the service can work. Public notes may be seen, saved or copied by other students while available.

5. Study groups and sessions

Group creators may manage group details, membership, discussions and sessions under the available admin tools. Admin powers must be used fairly and in line with our Community Guidelines.

Session details, attendance, study help and any arrangements between students are user-generated and are not guaranteed by Peermigo. Participants are responsible for communicating changes and respecting each other's time.

6. Prohibited technical activity

Do not attempt to bypass access controls, access private messages or files without permission, scrape personal data, exploit security vulnerabilities, flood the service with automated requests, distribute malware, or interfere with the platform's operation.

If you discover a vulnerability, report it privately to peermatchstudents@gmail.com rather than exploiting or publicly disclosing private student data.

7. Reports and moderation

Peermigo may investigate reports and take appropriate action such as removing content, limiting access, suspending an account, or cooperating with authorities where required. Moderation decisions depend on the circumstances and information available.

If you believe a decision was made in error, email the contact address below with relevant details.

8. External learning resources

Links to W3Schools, TutorialsPoint and other learning sites are provided for convenience. They are operated independently, and Peermigo does not control or guarantee their content, terms, pricing or availability.

9. Availability and responsibility

Peermigo is provided on an as-available basis. We work to maintain the service but cannot promise uninterrupted access, message delivery, file availability, compatibility, or that a study match will produce any particular academic outcome.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

10. Changes, questions and contact

We may update features or these terms as the platform develops. Check the date above for the latest revision and review material changes when announced.

Questions, reports and account concerns: peermatchstudents@gmail.com.
