OUR PURPOSE

Built around how students really learn.

Whether you're revising a tough module, looking for people at your campus, or sharing helpful notes, Peermigo helps you connect with students who want to grow together.

The platform brings peer discovery, group discussions, study sessions and shared resources into one place. Your student community is available after creating an account.
