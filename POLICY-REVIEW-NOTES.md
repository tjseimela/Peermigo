## Important: operator review still required
The text reflects the website features and the cited South African POPIA principles; it is not a legal compliance certification. Before release, the operator should confirm:
1. **Responsible party**: full legal/operator name, physical address, and appointed Information Officer/contact as legally applicable. Do not invent these details; add them to the Privacy Policy after confirmation.
2. **Retention**: actual retention/deletion schedules, backups, and response handling for reports and records; align wording with operational practice.
3. **Third parties and transfers**: current processor contracts, regions and possible secondary hosting, CDN and analytics settings.
4. **Safety operations**: moderation workflow and how quickly reports or privacy requests are handled. Do not claim guaranteed response times.
5. **Visibility**: the Notes Feed hides non-discoverable profiles in the UI, but public notes remain readable under database policies to authenticated users in some contexts. Do not promise that hiding a profile revokes every previously public note or downloaded file; use Only me for private notes. Consider a backend visibility policy if product expectations require a stronger rule.
6. **Consent/age**: independently verify age restrictions and enforcement before general launch.
7. **Registration**: live-test confirmation email, security notices, and account deletion behavior after deploying.

No database or Supabase changes were made during this documentation refresh.
